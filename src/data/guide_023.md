en: "BPP conducts its business with due consideration of potential impacts on society, the environment, and stakeholders across the value chain. BPP's stakeholder engagement practices are aligned with the international AA1000 Stakeholder Engagement Standard (AA10OOSES), which is founded on the principles of Inclusiveness, Materiality, and Responsiveness. These principles guide the Company in engaging all stakeholder groups, identifying material sustainability issues affecting both BPP and its stakeholders across the value chain, and responding to stakeholder expectations through transparent operations disclosure. In addition, BPP conducts an annual review of its stakeholder engagement activities and communication channels."
